### Witch I

A long listing used to roll off the top and vanish. Up walked the last lines you had typed, the way a shell does, and the lines themselves were gone. The glass keeps them now. Six hundred and sixty-six.

She wakes latched. That is the scroll lock, and the word is `LATCH`. At SPEAK, at AUTO, and while DEMAND waits, Up and Down walk what rolled away. Page Up and Page Down move a glassful. Home is the oldest line still kept. End sits on the pen. The bezel says GAZE and how far above the pen you are.

While a program runs, the glass is text. The arrows belong to the program. Output rolls the way a glass always did. When she returns to SPEAK, the latch is how you walk back up. `LATCH OFF` opens the glass, and Up recalls again. F7 does it. So does Scroll Lock.

The mouse is not plugged in. The pointer is hidden over the glass, and a click does nothing. The bezel is an emoji, a word, and a function key. It is not a row of buttons.
