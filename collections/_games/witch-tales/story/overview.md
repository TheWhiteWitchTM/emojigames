# A Witch's Tale — Overview

## Premise

You are a witch.  
The land has problems. You have opinions about them and a certain set of skills for dealing with them.

The story is told in chapters. Each chapter is a self-contained stretch of play that can be finished in one sitting, but the larger tale continues across them.

## Structure

- Chapters are numbered and live in separate folders.
- Shared systems (movement, combat, inventory, stats, saving) stay the same.
- Each chapter supplies its own maps, encounters, dialogue, and any new rules it needs.
- Your save remembers which chapter you are on and what you have done.

## Saving

The game stores your progress in the browser (local storage).  
You can continue where you left off as long as you use the same browser.

## Release pattern

One chapter is added at a time.  
When a chapter ends, the game checks for the next folder.  
If it is not there yet, you see a short message and can keep your save until the next part arrives.

## Tone and style

Clear, a little sharp, and grounded in the systems of the early tile-based RPGs.  
The witch is competent. The world is not always cooperative.
