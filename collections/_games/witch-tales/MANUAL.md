# A Witch's Tale

A chaptered tile tale. You are a witch. The land has problems. Alpha. Chapter 01 is a cottage, a yard, and the trees south of it.

## Start

Return, or N, begins a new tale. C continues, if this browser still has a save. R returns to the title.

The save keeps your chapter, your place, your health, and the flags you set. F5 writes it. When a chapter ends, the game looks for the next numbered folder. Chapter 02 is not written yet. The save waits.

## Keys

Arrows or WASD move.

- T, or Space, talks
- L looks
- I shows what you carry
- H, or F1, or `?`, prints this list

## A fight

1, or Return, is a strike. 2 is a spark, if you have one left. 3, or Escape, is a step back. If you fall, you wake in the bed. The flags you set stay set.

The iron key was on the nail. Something that should not be there took it into the woods. The far gate still wants that key.
