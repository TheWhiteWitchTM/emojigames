# Galaxy Hunt 3D

The same mission as Galaxy Hunt, written in Magic. The program is `galaxy-hunt-3d.magic`. 2d draws the charts. 3d is the quadrant you fly. ui is the bridge.

## Start

Play fills the screen. Escape brings the page back. Return leaves the title. R, when the mission ends, starts another and skips the title.

## The helm

Left and Right, or A and D, turn the course. Up or W moves one sector along that course. V switches the chart view and the chase view. Space opens the beams. M launches a missile. S sets the shields. Enter or C opens the bridge. 1 is the short-range scan. 2 is the long-range scan. H is the key card. Enter or Space on a chart returns to the helm.

## Course

1 is east. 2 is northeast. 3 is north. 4 is northwest. 5 is west. 6 is southwest. 7 is south. 8 is southeast. 9 is east again. The gold block sits ahead of the hunter. North is the far side of the quadrant. East is to the right in the chart view.

## Navigate

The bridge can warp. Course is 1 to 9. Warp is 0.1 to 8. A warp of 1 crosses about eight sectors. Stars stop the hunter, and they stop missiles. Flying onto an outpost docks. Energy, missiles, and the hull come back, and the shields drop. Moving off that sector leaves the bay. Raiders do not shoot while the hunter is docked.

## The map

🚀 is you. 👾 is a raider. ⭐ is a star. 🏢 is an outpost. A long-range scan remembers that neighborhood, so the computer can point at an outpost it has seen. Damage lasts until the next dock. Clear every raider before the energy or the stardate runs out.
