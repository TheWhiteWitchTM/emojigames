# Pale March

You are the White Witch. The Ash Choir kept five seals so the Unlit could not finish rising. They burned the other witches and left you the grimoire, the owl Quill, and the title. King Hollow is writing your true name into the gate. Close four marches before the name is finished.

Nothing here belongs to anyone else's army or anyone else's rulebook. The host, the seals, and the names are this march.

## Start

Play fills the screen. The true name starts as WINTER. Change it, or leave it. Begin walks out of Pale Hold. Continue picks up the march saved on this machine. R, after an ending, starts another march and skips the title.

Escape leaves the march and brings this page back. It is not a control on the field. The march is saved when you leave.

## The map

Pale Hold is the fire. Four roads leave it, and the gate road opens when the four seals are shut.

Arrows, or W and S, move through the list. Enter walks. You can also press a road, or the place itself, on the map. Rest fills your vitae before you go. If someone is walking with you, the list can call them. If you carry more than one relic, the list says which one is in your hand. Only the one you carry is working.

## The field

The picture stands up. W and S walk. A and D turn, and so do the arrows. Q and E step aside. The view stays behind you. Your speed stays on the way you are facing.

Space casts the spell in hand. Hold it and First Frost keeps coming. 1 is First Frost, a pale bolt. 2 is White Wake, a ring that hurts and shoves, and it spends two focus. 3 is Quill's Mark. The owl dives, and it spends one focus. Focus comes back on its own.

Shift is a short step through them. You are briefly elsewhere, and a hit in that moment does not land. After a real hit you flicker, and the next one misses for a beat.

P pauses. K pauses on the keys. M mutes Quill.

On a touch screen the left pad walks and turns. Cast is the right pad.

Ash hounds rush. Sergeants walk in and swing. Cinder priests stand off and throw fire. Banner-wights are slow and thick. The one the seal belongs to comes in after the rank in front of them is down.

## What you are owed, and what you spend

You start with ten vitae. Fall in a march and the grimoire brings you back to the fire at one vitae. That seal is still open. The fight starts over.

Fall at the gate and King Hollow tears a letter off your name. Three letters, and he finishes it. That is the end.

The lantern makes First Frost hit harder. The broken lantern slows the host. A burned page makes White Wake wider. The crown adds two vitae while you carry it, and the gate keeps an extra wight. Laying Bramble down adds one vitae for good, and it is not a relic you can put down.

Bramble's shade slows whoever stands near you. Sir Pell takes the front. He can fall. He is there again on the next field. Moth throws a bolt of her own, on her own time.

## The gate

What you did with Bramble, the lantern, the stacks, and the crown is still true when the gate shuts. The march ends on that, not on a score.
