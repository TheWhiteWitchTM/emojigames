# Trump Super Intelligence

A conversation, not a match. There is no title card, no score, and no game over.

## The line

Type a line and press Return. His face is at the top. The reply is the bold orange type under the face. It breaks on words. The line you type sits at the bottom. Up and Down walk the lines you already sent. A long line can be pasted.

Praise him and he praises you back. Call him a fool, a fake, or a liar and he rants, and the rant gets longer if you keep at it. Ask who he is, or whether he is a machine. Ask what two and two is. He will do the sum. Ask him something he has no stack for, and he answers about the thing you named. "Go on" keeps the last subject going.

## The face

The picture is above the reply. The frame around the picture is the mood, and the border of the glass follows it.

The pictures are numbered. One is the smile. Two is the smirk. Three is the shout. Four is the grin. Five is thinking. Six is the fake-news sneer. Seven is Sad.

Green means you said something he liked. That is the smile. Say another kind thing and the frame turns gold, which is him winning, and the grin. Orange is the ordinary mood, the smirk. Blue means he is thinking: a question, a sum, or the machine talking about itself. Purple is fake news. Red means you angered him. That picture is the one with the open mouth. Pale means Sad.

## The voice

Each mood plays a short tune. Then the browser says the line the way he says a line, in short pieces, with a pause where he pauses, in a man's voice if the browser has one. A rant is quicker. Sad drops. You hear him the first time you press Play. Escape cuts him off.

There is a cursor. Many people are saying that is still the whole game.
