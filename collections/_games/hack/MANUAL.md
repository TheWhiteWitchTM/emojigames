# Hack

Rooms carved out of rock, corridors between them, and a bestiary that gets meaner as you go down. The Amulet of Yendor is on the sixth floor. Picking it up is the win.

## Start

Return leaves the title. R starts again and skips the title.

## Keys

Arrows, WASD, or the vi keys, diagonals included: y u b n, and q e z c. `?` prints the reminder.

- `P` drinks a potion
- `Z` reads a scroll
- `I` prints the pack

## The floor

Step on a thing and it goes into your pack. A better weapon gets wielded. Armor takes the edge off a hit. Monsters walk toward you. If they reach you, they swing.

Your strength starts at 16. Your hit points start at 20. The floors stay where you left them.
