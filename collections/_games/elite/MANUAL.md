# Elite

You are the commander of a Cobra Mk III. Eight galaxies, 256 systems each. Buy, jump, fight, and dock. The picture is new. The flight model is the old one: your speed stays on the nose.

## Start

Play fills the screen. Type a name, or leave JAMESON, and press Return. Continue picks up the commander saved on this machine. R, after the ship is lost, starts another JAMESON and skips the title.

Escape leaves the cockpit and brings this page back. It is not a control in the ship.

## The stick

W and S, or the up and down arrows, pitch. Up raises the nose. A and D, or the left and right arrows, roll. Q and E yaw.

R and F, or = and −, change speed. The mouse wheel does it too. On a touch screen the left pad flies and the right pad is the throttle.

Speed runs from 0 to 36. You do not drift. Cut the throttle and the ship stops on its line.

1 is the front view, 2 the rear, 3 the left, 4 the right. The laser on that face is the one that fires. N cycles the compass through the station, the planet, and the sun. A green blip is ahead of the nose. A red blip is behind you.

P pauses. Z mutes. K lists the keys.

## The guns

Space fires. The pulse laser is already on the nose. A beam is faster and weaker. A military laser hits harder. A mining laser is for rock.

T arms a missile and cycles targets inside scanner range. M launches. U safes it. X fires the ECM and spends a little energy. B is the energy bomb. It kills ordinary ships nearby, and it does not touch a Thargoid. Clean ships in that blast make you a fugitive.

Shooting a clean trader or the police makes you an offender. Killing one makes you a fugitive. Pirates, hunters, and Thargoids are fair game, and their bounty is paid when they die. Your legal status halves when you arrive from a jump.

The ratings, by kills: Harmless, Mostly Harmless, Poor, Average, Above Average, Competent, Dangerous, Deadly, Elite.

## Docking

The station is a hexagon spinning in front of the planet. The slot faces away from the planet and turns with the station. The marker on the slot is green when your nose is lined up with it, and red when it is not.

Come in between speed 2 and 16, through the middle of the slot, rolled to match the station. Too fast, or crooked, and you scrape the bay. Fast enough and the ship is lost.

C turns on the docking computer when you are inside about 3.8 km and under speed 28. It flies the approach and plays while it does. C again turns it off. A scrape turns it off too.

## Trade

The market prices are in credits. Food at a quiet Lave is 3.6. One lot takes one tonne of the hold, even when the unit on the board is kilograms or grams. The hold starts at 20 tonnes. A large cargo bay makes it 35.

Right arrow, or Buy, takes one lot. Left arrow, or Sell, puts one back. Hold Shift to fill or empty the room you have. Contract goods will not sell.

Fuel is 0.2 credits per tenth of a light year, up to 7.0. Fill buys the tank.

Slaves and narcotics are illegal where the government is a confederacy or stricter. Firearms are illegal in a democracy or a corporate state. The police notice.

What a station sells depends on its tech level. Lave is tech 5, so the board there stops at fuel scoops. A new laser replaces the one on that mount. There is no refund.

## Jump

Set a target on the short chart or the galactic chart. Arrows move. Type the start of a name. Enter locks it. H starts an eight-second countdown and spends the fuel. H again aborts before you leave. You cannot jump to the system you are in, past 7 light years, or farther than the fuel you have.

About one jump in twenty misses and drops you in witchspace. The fuel is already gone. Thargoids are there. H works again after the drive finishes charging, and that jump is free.

G uses a galactic hyperdrive. It is gone after the one jump, and the next galaxy comes up. The eighth galaxy wraps back to the first.

An escape pod, if you have one, puts a stock Cobra back in the station. You keep the cash and the kills. You lose the cargo and the fittings. Your legal status is halved. Without a pod, the game is over and the save is wiped.

## Other fittings

Fuel scoops pick up cargo at speed 24 or less, and they skim fuel from the sun. Too fast and the canister breaks. Radioactives hurt. Fly too close to the sun and the cabin overheats. The planet's surface is the end of the ship.

A mining laser breaks asteroids into minerals. An energy bomb is a single shot. The extra energy unit refills the batteries faster. The docking computer and the galactic hyperdrive are for high-tech stations.

Trumbles are rare, they breed, and they eat what is in the hold. They die if the cabin gets hot. Jettison dumps them first, and after that it dumps one tonne.

A station may offer one delivery or one assassination. Accept it, jump, and finish it. Leave the goods behind and the contract fails. Kill the named ship and the reward is the bounty.

The commander is saved when you dock, when you buy, when you arrive, and when Escape brings you back to this page. Opening that save puts you in the station, not in deep space.
