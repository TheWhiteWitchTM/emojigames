# Witch Hunt

Religious symbols chase the witch. Eat every pellet. Ten levels. Alpha.

## Start

Return leaves the title. R plays again after you clear the tenth level, or after you are caught. P pauses. Three lives. The best score stays in this browser.

## The maze

Arrows or WASD move. Space casts a spell, and a spell spends energy. A crystal turns the hunt: while the shield is up, the hunters are slower, and she can catch them.

Level 1 gives you time to turn. Each later level is a little quicker. The hunters stay slower than the witch in a straight hall, until the late levels close that gap. Running home after a catch is quick.
