# Rogue

Six floors. Rooms, corridors, and the Amulet of Yendor at the bottom. You have to carry it back up to the road home.

## Start

Return leaves the title. R starts a new run from floor one, including after you die. There is no save. A dead run is a dead run.

## The delve

Arrows or WASD move a step. Walk into a monster and you hit it. Stand next to one and it hits you back.

Gold adds to the purse when you step on it. A potion goes into your pack. Q drinks one and heals.

Stairs go down. Floor six has the amulet. Pick it up, climb, and the road home is the win. Hit points start at 20.
