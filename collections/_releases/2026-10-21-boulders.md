# 🪨 Boulders

{% include release-banners.html %}

21 October 2026. A cave you can see.

[Boulders]({{ '/games/boulders/' | relative_url }}) is three holes. You dig. A rock falls when the cell under it is empty, and it rolls off another rock when the side is clear. Diamonds are the count. The door opens when the count is met. Walk through it.

The first hole is the lesson. The boxed stone has a rock sitting over a diamond, and a way around. The loose roof falls if you dig the cell under a rock. Take those diamonds from the side.

Arrows or WASD. Three lives. A fall spends one and puts that cave back. Return starts. R, after the end, starts from the first hole.

Play fills the screen. Escape brings this page back.
