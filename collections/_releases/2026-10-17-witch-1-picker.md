# 🖥️ Witch I points

17 October 2026. The box learned to point.

`SHELF` opens the programs on the glass. Up and down move the mark. Enter takes that one down and does not run it. She tells you that a number changes a line. Escape leaves the shelf where it is.

`LIST` prints the program. The line numbers are yellow. Retype one, and that line is the new line. `REVEAL` still prints them with no color and no lecture.

`ANSI-WITCH` is a picture. `VEIL` sends the glass its own color codes: the sixteen, the 256, and a color mixed on the spot. The lines are in `shelf/ansi-witch.wasic`. Change a number, run it again.
