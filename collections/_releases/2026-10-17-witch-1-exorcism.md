# 🖥️ Witch I, the exorcism

17 October 2026. She draws herself before she speaks.

When the glass wakes, the ANSI witch from `ANSI-WITCH` paints in, hat first. The sixteen, the 256, and a color mixed on the spot. Two low notes, and the drawing steps aside for the portrait and the count.

Under the glass, six keys sit beside the lines in use. LIST, RUN, SHELF, SCRUB, MAGIC, EXORCISE. The chords are Alt+Shift and a letter. A browser does not spend those on tabs, reload, or the address line. MAGIC numbers the next lines. AUTO is the same word.

EXORCISE rewrites a BASIC listing into WASIC. PRINT becomes CHANT, GOTO becomes FLYTO, FOR becomes CURSE, and the rest of the old names follow. INT, SIN, RND, and the other sums stay. PEEK answers 0. A line she still cannot say is buried as a whisper, and RUN steps over it. An empty circle opens the desk for a `.BAS` file. She leaves the lines sitting there. You run them.
