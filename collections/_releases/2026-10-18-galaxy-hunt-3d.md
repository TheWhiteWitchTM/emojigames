# 🚀 Galaxy Hunt 3D

{% include release-banners.html %}

18 October 2026. [Galaxy Hunt 3D]({{ '/games/galaxy-hunt-3d/' | relative_url }}) is the Super Star Trek mission, written in Magic.

2d draws the charts. 3d is the quadrant. ui is the bridge. The gold block ahead of the hunter is the course. Dock by flying onto an outpost, and leave by flying off it. Return leaves the title. R, after the end, starts another and skips the title. Play fills the screen. Escape brings the page back.
