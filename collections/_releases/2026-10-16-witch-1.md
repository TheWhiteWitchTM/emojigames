# 🖥️ Witch I

16 October 2026. She woke up.

[Witch I]({{ '/games/witch-1/' | relative_url }}) is a small computer on a black glass. Forty columns, twenty-four rows, a line number, and SPEAK.

The language is WASIC. `CHANT` writes a line. `DEMAND` asks. `CURSE` and `BREAK` count. `FLYTO` jumps. A line with a number is stored, junk included. A BASIC word fails when she reaches it. PEEK, POKE, and SYS make her complain. She is Witch I. One.

`SHELF` lists what shipped in the box: HELLO, GUESS, DICE, OMEN, CIRCLES, MINIONS, KEYS. `UNSEAL "shelf:GUESS"` loads one. `RUN` starts it. Escape stops it.

`SEAL` keeps a program in this browser. `GRIMOIRE` prints the words.
