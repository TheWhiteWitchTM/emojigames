# 🖥️ Witch ][

16 October 2026. She woke up.

[Witch \][]({{ '/games/witch-1/' | relative_url }}) is a small computer on a black glass. Forty columns, twenty-four rows, a line number, and SPEAK.

The language is WASIC. `CHANT` writes a line. `DEMAND` asks. `CURSE` and `BREAK` count. `FLYTO` jumps. A BASIC word is stored if you insist, and it fails when she reaches it. She says so.

`SHELF` lists what shipped in the box: HELLO, GUESS, DICE, OMEN, CIRCLES, MINIONS, KEYS. `UNSEAL "shelf:GUESS"` loads one. `RUN` starts it. Escape stops it.

`SEAL` keeps a program in this browser. `GRIMOIRE` prints the words.
