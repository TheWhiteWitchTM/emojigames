17 October 2026. A computer for learning.

[Basic I]({{ '/games/basic-1/' | relative_url }}) is the other 40-column machine. Forty columns, twenty-four rows, and room for 512 lines. The prompt is READY. A line with a number is stored. A line without one runs now.

MODULES opens the lessons that shipped in the box. Getting Started is the first hour. The tutorial walks the rest. The manual is every word this computer knows.

Boot fills the screen. Escape ends the session and brings the page back. Boot again and the lines you stored are still there. A program that was running has stopped.

She is not this machine. Witch I speaks WASIC and keeps 666 lines. Basic I says READY, and the lessons are the point.
