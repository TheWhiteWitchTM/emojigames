22 October 2026. The flagship.

[Carriers]({{ '/games/carriers/' | relative_url }}) is seven battles from the Pacific war. Coral Sea, Midway, the Eastern Solomons, Santa Cruz, the Philippine Sea, Cape Engaño, and Pearl Harbor. Pick a side. The loading plate comes up, then the list.

Click one of yours. Click one of theirs to send the strike. Click a square to move. Commit. Fighters stay over their own ship. A ship you cannot see cannot be struck.
