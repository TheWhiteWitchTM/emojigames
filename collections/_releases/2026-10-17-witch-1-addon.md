# 🖥️ Witch I, the add-on

{% include release-banners.html %}

17 October 2026. The same computer. The box learned a few more tricks.

She shipped on the 16th. This is not another Witch I. The programs left the script and moved into `shelf/`, and the extension is `.wasic`. `SHELF` reads the catalog and opens it on the glass. Up and down move the mark. Enter takes that program down and does not run it. She tells you that a number changes a line. Escape leaves the shelf where it is.

`HELLO-WITCH` and `HELLO-DEMON` are the two hellos. CAROL hums. FAMILIAR paces. `ANSI-WITCH` is a picture. `VEIL` sends the glass its own color codes: the sixteen, the 256, and a color mixed on the spot. `LIST` prints the program. The line numbers are yellow. Retype one, and that line is the new line.

When the glass wakes, she draws herself in those colors, hat first. Two low notes, and the drawing steps aside for the portrait and the count. Then the words roll up and the glass says SPEAK.

Under the glass, the keys are an emoji, a word, and a function key. LIST, RUN, SHELF, SCRUB, MAGIC, EXORCISE, LATCH. Nothing there is a button. She wakes latched. Up walks the lines that rolled off the glass. `LATCH OFF` opens it. F7 and Scroll Lock do what `LATCH` does.

EXORCISE rewrites a BASIC listing into WASIC. PRINT becomes CHANT, GOTO becomes FLYTO, FOR becomes CURSE, and the rest of the old names follow. INT, SIN, RND, and the other sums stay. PEEK answers 0. A line she still cannot say is buried as a whisper, and RUN steps over it. An empty circle opens the desk for a `.BAS` file. She leaves the lines sitting there. You run them.

A refusal is red, with a mark, and two notes going down. The walls she keeps are in [SYSTEM.md]({{ '/SYSTEM.md' | relative_url }}).
