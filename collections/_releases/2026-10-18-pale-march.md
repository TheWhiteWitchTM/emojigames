18 October 2026. The witch leaves Pale Hold.

[Pale March]({{ '/games/pale-march/' | relative_url }}) is the campaign. You are the White Witch. Quill keeps the roads. King Hollow is writing your true name into the Unlit Gate, and four seals are still open. The map is the flat engine. The field stands up: ground, ruins, and the host in front of you.

The marches are Whisper Wood, the Salt Stair, the Burned Choir, and the Barrow Kings. What you do when a seal opens walks with you to the gate. Fall in a march and the grimoire brings you home. Fall at the gate three times and the name is finished.

Play fills the screen. Escape brings this page back. The march stays on this machine.
