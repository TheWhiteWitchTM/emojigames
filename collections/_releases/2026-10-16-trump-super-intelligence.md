# 🤖 Trump Super Intelligence

16 October 2026. The rest of the shelf.

[Trump Super Intelligence]({{ '/games/trump-si/' | relative_url }}) is not a port, and it is not a model. You type a line, press Enter, and it answers from a pile of written replies. The line you typed is matched against a list of words. Praise, insults, China, the news, winning, a greeting: each one has its own stack of answers, and the game picks one.

The face above the text is part of the answer. Five pictures. Happy, smug, ranting, winning, and a neutral that does not get much use, because the default mood is smug. Call it great and the face softens. Call it a liar, a fake, or a fool and it rants, and the little square-wave noise drops in pitch like something powering down in a bad mood.

There is no score and no win. There is a cursor. Many people are saying that is the whole game.

That is the shelf as of today. [Pong]({{ '/games/pong/' | relative_url }}) through [Hack]({{ '/games/hack/' | relative_url }}) are the four days before this one. The [catalog]({{ '/games/' | relative_url }}) has all eight.
