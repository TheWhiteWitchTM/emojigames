# 🧙 A Witch's Tale

{% include release-banners.html %}

16 October 2026. A chapter, and a save.

[A Witch's Tale]({{ '/games/witch-tales/' | relative_url }}) is a tile RPG told in parts. You are a witch. The land has problems. The first chapter is a cottage, a yard, and the trees south of it.

You wake with very little. The wards on the door have gone quiet. A crow at the window has opinions. Something that should not be there took the iron key off the nail and went into the woods. The far gate still wants that key.

Arrows or WASD move. T talks. L looks. Combat is a strike, a spark, or a step back. If you fall, you wake in the bed. The flags you set stay set.

The game stores your chapter, your place, your health, and those flags in this browser. When a chapter ends it looks for the next numbered folder. Chapter 02 is not written yet. The save waits.

That is ten games on the shelf.
