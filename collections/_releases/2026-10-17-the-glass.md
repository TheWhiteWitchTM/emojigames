# 🕹️ The glass

17 October 2026. Return to begin. R to go again.

The playable games now draw on the emoji engine. Each one, except the conversation, opens on a title. Return leaves it. If you win, or if you die, the last screen says so, and R starts that game again without the title.

[Trump Super Intelligence]({{ '/games/trump-si/' | relative_url }}) is not a match. It has no title and no ending. You type, it answers, the face follows the mood.

## Featured

The Games menu carries three. The [catalog]({{ '/games/' | relative_url }}) still has the rest, with those three first.

[Galaxy Hunt]({{ '/games/galaxy-hunt/' | relative_url }}) is the starship. Eight by eight, raiders, outposts, and a stardate. Dock to repair. `HELP` is the manual.

[The Oregon Trail]({{ '/games/oregon-trail/' | relative_url }}) keeps the trail it already had. The new screen is only the title in front of it. Return still means Return, once you are on the road.

[Hack]({{ '/games/hack/' | relative_url }}) is the dungeon with rooms and a bestiary. A better weapon gets wielded. Armor soaks a hit. `P` drinks a potion. The Amulet of Yendor is on the sixth floor, and picking it up is the win.

## The others

[Pong]({{ '/games/pong/' | relative_url }}) and [Snake]({{ '/games/snake/' | relative_url }}) are no longer a text box. Paddles, a ball, a snake, apples, and the furniture. Snake clears the board at one hundred.

[Rogue]({{ '/games/rogue/' | relative_url }}) is the shorter delve: rooms, corridors, six floors, the amulet at the bottom. `Q` drinks.

[Adventureland]({{ '/games/adventureland/' | relative_url }}) wants the thirteen treasures in the stump. The bees, the dragon, the bear, and the hole will end it if you insist.

[Pirate Adventure]({{ '/games/pirate/' | relative_url }}) wants the three treasures back in the London flat. The skeleton does not share, unless the parrot has heard the words.
